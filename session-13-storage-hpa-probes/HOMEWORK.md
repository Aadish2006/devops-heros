# Homework: Storage, Autoscaling, and Probes

1. Configure a PersistentVolumeClaim and mount it into an application Pod. Verify that data survives a Pod replacement.
2. Configure readiness and liveness probes, then describe how their effects differ.
3. Configure a HorizontalPodAutoscaler for a Deployment and observe its status under load where metrics are available.
4. Explain what happens when a claim cannot bind or a workload has no resource requests for autoscaling.

**Deliverable:** Manifests, verification output, and notes on storage and scaling behavior.
